# [2.3.0] - 2026-11-25

The site takes on the look of Herdr. The GitHub calendar and the open source
figures are drawn as dithered charts after Monocode's, and the calendar's days
can now be read from the keyboard and with a tap. The picture viewer, the
cookie preferences and the menus open the way Herdr's modals do, and give the
focus back where it came from, in Safari too. The favicon, a new iOS app icon
and the project logos sit on the same dithered tile as in Herdr. Under the
surface, the hover pixels are drawn by the GPU, a slow first content page no
longer freezes the screen, Google Analytics counts each page under its own
title, and the deploy pins the VPS's host key instead of scanning for it.

### Added

- **Monocode's dither charts**
  ([#165](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/165)). The GitHub
  calendar (home, `/activity`) is drawn as a dithered heatmap, after
  Monocode's `/visualize` (MIT): a day's count becomes a share of inked 3px
  cells through a 4x4 Bayer matrix. Its days can now be read from the keyboard
  (arrow keys, said aloud) and with a tap, not only under the mouse, and the
  last twelve months gain a sparkline of their monthly totals. The open
  source card (`/activity`, `/parcours`) shows each figure's statuses as a
  dithered ring.
  `UiDitherChart` draws them with the charts' shared core, a verbatim copy, in
  the theme's colours (light, dark, blur), and lands them at once when motion
  is reduced or paused.
- **An app icon, and the favicon on a dithered tile**
  ([#169](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/169)). Adding the site to
  an iOS home screen used a screenshot of the page: `apple-touch-icon.png`
  (180 px, opaque and square to the edge, iOS rounds it itself) now stands in.
  It and the favicon put the K on the tile of the project logos in Herdr, a
  ramp from `#505050` at the top left to `#262626` at the bottom right in four
  tones dithered by a Bayer matrix. The favicon keeps the rounded corners and
  no longer follows the system's colour scheme. The pages keep the bare K; the
  repository banner takes the tile.
- **Project logos on their dithered tile**
  ([#171](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/171)). On the home's
  pinned repositories and on `/apps`, a repository with a logo shows it on its
  tile, in place of the bordered box and at its size: gwm-cli and the K of
  kbrdn.dev, dark and light, on the same ramps as the favicon (the site's own
  copies, `public/images/tiles`: gwm-cli's default branch does not have its
  tile yet), and dotfiles, newly listed, with its own orange tile. The banner
  of the gwm article and its Open Graph image, and the poster on the wall of the
  `/apps` hero, take gwm's tile too.

### Changed

- **Modals and popups in the look of the Herdr modals**
  ([#167](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/167)). The picture viewer
  (an article's pictures, `/experience`'s illustrations) is now a card with a
  60px head over a pixel dither band tinted by its context (the blog's blue in
  an article, the site's orange elsewhere): a mark, the picture's title, its
  file name and size in mono, the close button with its key. The picture sits
  in a block, Esc is recalled in the foot, and a picture that fails to load is
  explained. It opens with a fade and a 6px rise in 180ms; on the way out the
  card leaves before the veil, and the focus is given back at once. The cookie
  preferences and banner and the home's profile cards take the same head, and
  the logo menu opens with the same motion.

### Fixed

- **The GitHub calendar's dates west of UTC**
  ([#165](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/165)). A day's tooltip
  read its date in the reader's timezone, a day early west of UTC, and the
  monthly view filed each month's first day under the month before. Both now
  read the date as GitHub gives it.
- **Google Analytics page views on client-side navigation.** GA's own
  history-based page view fired as the address changed, before Nuxt had
  rendered the next page: up to 7 s before on a first visit to `/blog`, which
  downloads the content database, so the page was counted under the previous
  page's title. The cookie plugin now sends the page view itself, once the new
  page and its title are on screen. A clicked row with no heading (the home's
  latest posts) is labelled with a space between its date and its title.
- **The first client-side navigation to a content page**
  ([#160](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/160)). It loads Nuxt
  Content's database in the browser, 2.6 to 7 s cold in prod, and the screen
  stayed frozen on an emptied page meanwhile: the view transition held the
  paint until the new page was ready, until Chrome gave up after 4 s. A view
  transition still waiting after 600 ms is now dropped, and a thin bar in the
  site's orange runs along the top (`NuxtLoadingIndicator`). Warm navigations,
  130 to 480 ms, keep their transition. The database dumps are served
  `immutable` (their address carries the content's checksum), and a
  Cloudflare Cache Rule lets the edge keep them and the wasm
  (`docs/CONFIGURATION.md`).
- **Lighter hover pixels on large cards**
  ([#153](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/153)). A card on /apps
  holds about 17,000 cells, and each frame rebuilt a canvas path out of every
  one of them. They are now drawn by the GPU in a single call (WebGL2, one
  context shared by the whole page), with the same shapes, colours and
  animation. Drawing that card's cells takes 0.3 to 0.5 ms of script per frame
  instead of 1.6 to 3.7; on the page, hovering it with the circle shape costs
  about half the main-thread and GPU time it did. A browser without WebGL2
  (hardware acceleration turned off) still draws them the old way.
- **The deploy no longer depends on `ssh-keyscan`**
  ([#42](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/42),
  [#163](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/163)). The `Setup ssh`
  step of `deploy.yml` and `release.yml` fetched the VPS's host key at deploy
  time, and `ssh-keyscan`'s silent timeouts failed the v1.0.0 and v1.0.1
  releases with nothing to go on. The key is now pinned in the `VPS_HOST_KEY`
  secret, read on the VPS itself, and the deploy's `ssh` checks it strictly:
  one SSH connection per deploy instead of up to six. A missing or malformed
  secret, a changed host key or an unreachable VPS each fail with their reason
  in the log, the host, its resolved addresses and its key's fingerprint
  masked as `***`.
- **Focus and motion in the overlays**
  ([#167](https://github.com/kbrdn1/kbrdn.dev/issues/167)). The site's pause
  control now stills a modal's veil too (`::backdrop`). Escape in the mobile
  menu and in the logo menu left the focus on the page's body; it goes back to
  the menu's button. The article's mobile contents close on Escape too,
  focusing their button. Closing the picture viewer gives the focus back to the
  picture's button, in Safari too (a click does not focus a button there), and
  the viewer is named by its visible title, never by an empty alt. For the same
  reason, closing the cookie preferences opened with the mouse in Safari left
  the focus on the body; it goes back to the footer's "Cookies" button.
